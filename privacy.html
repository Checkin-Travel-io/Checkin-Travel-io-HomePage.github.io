Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-15

This website is a “coming soon” landing page for Checkin Travel (the “Site”). We respect privacy and aim to collect only the minimum data needed to operate the Site and respond to inquiries.

Applicable framework

For activities in Israel, we follow the general principles reflected in Israel’s privacy framework, including the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 and the Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017.

Information we collect

  • Contact emails: If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you send.
  • Technical logs: Basic server logs (e.g., IP address, user-agent, timestamps) for security and reliability.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested updates.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site (including monitoring abuse and troubleshooting).

Cookies and analytics

The Site may use essential cookies and limited measurement tools. If we add non-essential analytics or third-party tools, we will update this policy accordingly.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers (e.g., hosting, email) and share limited data with them only as required to operate the Site, under appropriate safeguards.

Data security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, such as access controls and security monitoring.

Retention

We retain contact emails and logs only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and then delete or anonymize them where feasible.

Your rights

You may request to review and correct personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable law.
